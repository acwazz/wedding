# Product Context

## Why
Personal wedding invitation site. Replaces paper invite info: program, venue, RSVP collection.

## Audience
Wedding guests, non-technical, mixed devices. Italian speakers.

## UX goals
- Elegant, warm, serif typography; palette = gold (#8d6f2d), sand (#d1c4a7), near-black (#0e1618), white, teal (#1b555f)
- One page, scroll + anchor nav, sticky header
- RSVP flow minimal friction: name, attend yes/no, guests count, dietary prefs, notes

## Success
Guests can find time/place info fast and submit RSVP. (Note: RSVP not persisted anywhere yet — see progress.md.)
